Abstract

Van Rossum tested his rule with three probes: a biased input, a change of input rate, and a long run to equilibrium. We ran the same probes in the rate-matched exp022 TR02 pair (PING and COBA, 25 Hz activity ceiling, seed 42), with plastic sensory weights. In the biased stream, PING saw only digit 3 for 10 s, then the balanced stream. Over the biased phase, test accuracy on digit 3 went from 77% to 68% with STDP alone and to 2% with STDP + scaling; on the other digits it went to 90% and 76%. In the rate-step stream, the input rate was halved after 5 s. The frozen network's E rate fell from 15 to 10.7 Hz and its rhythm frequency from 70 to 54 Hz. At the end, the E rate was 11.8 Hz with STDP alone and 12.8 Hz with STDP + scaling (setpoint 15.3 Hz). In the 60-s stream, the weights' correlation with the trained matrix relaxed with τ 7 s in PING and 9 s in COBA. One seed per probe, without uncertainty estimates.

  6. Competition. In the biased stream, the digit’s pixels are those whose mean intensity over the biased-phase images exceeds 0.25. Their mean outgoing X→E weight is compared with that of all other pixels; test accuracy is split into images of the biased digit and all others.
