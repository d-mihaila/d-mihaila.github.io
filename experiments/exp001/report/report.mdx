## Abstract

{data ? <p>How does van Rossum plasticity change a trained gamma-rhythmic network compared with a trained non-rhythmic one? We took the exp022 TR01 networks (no rate penalty, seed {String(cfg.seed)}, final epoch): PING, with its trained E→I→E loop, and COBA, without inhibition. Each network received the same continuous stream of {cfg.presentations} MNIST validation digits ({seconds} s, no state reset) twice: once with frozen weights and once with van Rossum STDP plus synaptic scaling on its 784×1,024 sensory weights. By the end, the mean absolute weight change was {change('ping')}% of the initial mean weight in PING and {change('coba')}% in COBA. Excitatory rates moved from {pct(s['ping/van_rossum'].e_rate_first_hz)} to {pct(s['ping/van_rossum'].e_rate_last_hz)} Hz in PING and from {pct(s['coba/van_rossum'].e_rate_first_hz)} to {pct(s['coba/van_rossum'].e_rate_last_hz)} Hz in COBA (first versus last {cfg.isi_block_presentations * cfg.presentation_ms / 1000} s). The PING rhythm became sharper: its rhythmicity contrast (the exp054 autocorrelogram metric, 0 for flat firing) went from {pct(s['ping/van_rossum'].rhythm_contrast_first, 2)} to {pct(s['ping/van_rossum'].rhythm_contrast_last, 2)} with van Rossum, against {pct(s['ping/frozen'].rhythm_contrast_first, 2)} to {pct(s['ping/frozen'].rhythm_contrast_last, 2)} with frozen weights; COBA stayed near {pct(s['coba/frozen'].rhythm_contrast_last, 2)}. Test accuracy on {cfg.accuracy_images.toLocaleString('en-GB')} official MNIST images went from {pct(s['ping/frozen'].accuracy_percent)}% to {pct(s['ping/van_rossum'].accuracy_percent)}% in PING and from {pct(s['coba/frozen'].accuracy_percent)}% to {pct(s['coba/van_rossum'].accuracy_percent)}% in COBA. This is one seed, one stream and one rule setting: a first diagnostic, not a parameter sweep.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}

## Discussion

### Main claim

- Does van Rossum plasticity act differently in a trained PING network than in COBA? One paragraph, strongest numbers first.

### Mechanism

- Why could the PING rhythm sharpen? For example: does STDP strengthen inputs that make E cells fire early in each cycle?
- Why would accuracy fall and then partly recover?

### Objections

- Rate confound: COBA fires about ten times faster, so it gets about ten times more spike pairings. What can this experiment not separate?

### Limitations

- One seed (42) and one stream order: no uncertainty estimates.
- Synaptic scaling is compressed in time 100x (tau 1 s, not 100 s); STDP is not.
- Only the sensory weights X->E are plastic; E->I, I->E and the readout are fixed.
- TR01 networks; MNIST validation digits as the stream.

### Next steps

- exp002: rate-matched PING and COBA. exp003: plasticity parameters as rhythm dials. exp004: the inhibitory time constant. exp005: van Rossum's own tests.
