## Abstract

{data ? <p>Van Rossum tested his rule with three probes: a biased input, a change of input rate, and a long run to equilibrium. We ran the same probes in the rate-matched exp022 TR02 pair (PING and COBA, 25 Hz activity ceiling, seed {String(cfg.seed)}), with plastic sensory weights. In the biased stream, PING saw only digit {String(cfg.biased_digit)} for {sec(cfg.biased_presentations / 2)} s, then the balanced stream. Over the biased phase, test accuracy on digit {String(cfg.biased_digit)} went from {f(s['ping__biased__stdp'].digit_accuracy_first, 0)}% to {f(s['ping__biased__stdp'].digit_accuracy_phase, 0)}% with STDP alone and to {f(s['ping__biased__both'].digit_accuracy_phase, 0)}% with STDP + scaling; on the other digits it went to {f(s['ping__biased__stdp'].other_accuracy_phase, 0)}% and {f(s['ping__biased__both'].other_accuracy_phase, 0)}%. In the rate-step stream, the input rate was halved after {sec(cfg.rate_step_presentations / 2)} s. The frozen network's E rate fell from {f(s['ping__rate_step__frozen'].rate_hz_pre, 1)} to {f(s['ping__rate_step__frozen'].rate_hz_post, 1)} Hz and its rhythm frequency from {f(s['ping__rate_step__frozen'].rhythm_frequency_hz_pre, 0)} to {f(s['ping__rate_step__frozen'].rhythm_frequency_hz_post, 0)} Hz. At the end, the E rate was {f(s['ping__rate_step__stdp'].rate_hz_end, 1)} Hz with STDP alone and {f(s['ping__rate_step__both'].rate_hz_end, 1)} Hz with STDP + scaling (setpoint {f(targets?.ping?.rate_step, 1)} Hz). In the {sec(cfg.long_presentations)}-s stream, the weights' correlation with the trained matrix relaxed with τ {tau('ping__long__both')} in PING and {tau('coba__long__both')} in COBA. One seed per probe, without uncertainty estimates.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}

## Discussion

### Main claim

- Which of van Rossum's results hold inside a PING network: (a) competition and forgetting, (b) rate homeostasis, (c) relaxation to a skewed equilibrium?

### Mechanism

- Does the rhythm protect the trained weights? Compare PING and COBA forgetting times at the same rate.

### Objections

- Per-class accuracy rests on about 50 test images per digit.
- A tau longer than the stream is only a lower bound.

### Limitations

- One seed (42) and one stream order: no uncertainty estimates.
- Synaptic scaling is compressed in time 100x (tau 1 s, not 100 s); STDP is not.
- Only the sensory weights X->E are plastic; E->I, I->E and the readout are fixed.
- One biased digit; one rate step. The 100x scaling compression sets the homeostatic time scale in these seconds.

### Next steps

- Other digits and step sizes; longer streams if tau exceeds 60 s; more seeds.
