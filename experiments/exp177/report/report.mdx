## Abstract

{data ? <p>Does the PING rhythm itself change what van Rossum plasticity does, or only the firing rate? exp176 could not tell, because COBA fired ten times faster than PING. Here both networks were trained with the same 25 Hz activity ceiling (exp022 TR02, seed {String(cfg.seed)}): PING started at {f(s['ping__frozen'].rate_hz_first, 1)} Hz and COBA at {f(s['coba__frozen'].rate_hz_first, 1)} Hz. Each received the same {cfg.presentations} MNIST test digits ({seconds} s, no state reset) under four rules on its sensory weights: frozen, STDP only, synaptic scaling only, and both (the full van Rossum model). With both, PING's rhythmicity contrast went from {f(s['ping__both'].rhythm_contrast_first)} to {f(s['ping__both'].rhythm_contrast_last)} (frozen: {f(s['ping__frozen'].rhythm_contrast_first)} to {f(s['ping__frozen'].rhythm_contrast_last)}), its rhythm frequency from {f(s['ping__both'].rhythm_frequency_hz_first, 0)} to {f(s['ping__both'].rhythm_frequency_hz_last, 0)} Hz, and the share of E cells firing per cycle from {pct(s['ping__both'].participation_first)}% to {pct(s['ping__both'].participation_last)}%. Test accuracy went from {f(s['ping__both'].accuracy_first, 1)}% to {f(s['ping__both'].accuracy_last, 1)}% in PING and from {f(s['coba__both'].accuracy_first, 1)}% to {f(s['coba__both'].accuracy_last, 1)}% in COBA; the mean absolute weight change reached {pct(s['ping__both'].weight_change_last)}% and {pct(s['coba__both'].weight_change_last)}% of the trained mean. One seed and one stream: a first comparison, not a sweep.</p> : <p>No completed evidence is selected.</p>}

## Discussion

### Main claim

- At the same E rate, is PING more or less robust to van Rossum plasticity than COBA? Does the rhythm itself matter, or was exp176's difference only rate?

### Mechanism

- What does each half of the rule do? Van Rossum: STDP alone has no competition and the rate follows the input; scaling adds homeostasis and competition.
- How could cycle-locked spike timing limit or focus spike pairing in PING?

### Objections

- PING rt25hz already has contrast near 1, so sharpening cannot show here. Is that a ceiling?
- The two networks also differ in trained weights, not only in the E-I loop.

### Limitations

- One seed (42) and one stream order: no uncertainty estimates.
- Synaptic scaling is compressed in time 100x (tau 1 s, not 100 s); STDP is not.
- Only the sensory weights X->E are plastic; E->I, I->E and the readout are fixed.
- TR02 networks trained on 7,000 images; a 10-s stream.

### Next steps

- More seeds; the rt10hz pair; plastic E->I and I->E.
