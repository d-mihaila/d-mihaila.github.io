## Abstract

{data ? <p>Can a plasticity parameter make the PING rhythm sharper, sparser, faster or slower? We took the trained exp022 TR01 PING network (seed {String(cfg.seed)}, no rate penalty; frozen rhythmicity contrast {f(s['frozen'].rhythm_contrast_first)}, rhythm frequency {f(s['frozen'].rhythm_frequency_hz_first, 0)} Hz) and ran the full van Rossum rule on its sensory weights for {cfg.presentations} MNIST test digits ({seconds} s), changing one parameter at a time. DISCUSSION TEMPLATE. To use: delete this block's first and last lines and replace

## Discussion

### Main claim

- Which plasticity parameter, if any, steers the PING rhythm, and in which direction?

### Mechanism

- Window against period: short windows pair spikes within one cycle; long windows also pair across cycles.
- Setpoint: scaling sets the E rate, and in PING the rate sets the frequency.

### Objections

- Equal-area scaling: a short window also has larger amplitudes. Is the effect timing or amplitude?
- tau20 is exp176's rule on a different stream. Do the two agree?

### Limitations

- One seed (42) and one stream order: no uncertainty estimates.
- Synaptic scaling is compressed in time 100x (tau 1 s, not 100 s); STDP is not.
- Only the sensory weights X->E are plastic; E->I, I->E and the readout are fixed.
- One parameter at a time: no interactions. Only two setpoints.

### Next steps

- A finer sweep where effects appear; more seeds; combine the best dial with exp179's networks.

*/}
