## Discussion

### Main claim

- Does the kind of PING rhythm change what van Rossum plasticity does, beyond the rate it brings?

### Mechanism

- The 20-ms STDP window against the rhythm period: how many pairings fall inside one cycle at each tau_GABA?

### Objections

- E rate co-varies with tau_GABA, and the pairing proxy is only approximate. What would equalise rates (for example the scaling setpoint)?
- Most networks have contrast near 1: is there room to sharpen?

### Limitations

- One seed (42) and one stream order: no uncertainty estimates.
- Synaptic scaling is compressed in time 100x (tau 1 s, not 100 s); STDP is not.
- Only the sensory weights X->E are plastic; E->I, I->E and the readout are fixed.
- TR03 networks trained on 7,000 images; a 10-s stream.

### Next steps

- Rate-equalised comparison; more seeds; link to exp178's window sweep (window/period ratio from the other side).
